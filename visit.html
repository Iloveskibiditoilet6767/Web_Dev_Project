  1. Saint Sophia's Church

    Saint Sophia Cathedral in Harbin is a former Russian Orthodox church built to boost military morale that now stands as a symbol of China's 20th-century cultural integration. Consecrated to the "Holy Wisdom of God" (the translation of Saint Sophia), the landmark represents both spiritual fortitude and the unique multicultural heritage of Harbin.

    1907(Built) ---- 1923–1932 (Expansion) ---- 1997–Present (Museum)




  2. Central Street

    Central Street (known as Zhongyang Dajie or Central Avenue) in Harbin, China, is Asia’s longest pedestrian street, stretching 1,450 meters through the Daoli District from the Songhua River Flood Control Memorial Tower to Jingwei Street.

    1898 – 1920s: The Russian Era & Origins ---- 1920s – 1940s: The Golden Age of Cosmopolitanism ---- 1950s – 1990s: Modernization & Preservation ---- 2000s – Present: Global Tourism Hub




  3. Harbin International Ice and Snow World

    The Harbin International Ice and Snow World spans over 800,000 square meters and is constructed using roughly 400,000 cubic meters of ice and snow. The blocks are harvested directly from the nearby Songhua River and carved using swing saws, chisels, and ice picks. There are multi-story ice castles illuminated by millions of colorful LED lights, a 500-meter super ice slide, the Snowflake Ferris Wheel, and an exclusive Frozen-themed zone via Disney China.

    Time Open
    Late Nov Early Dec Late Dec Early Jan Mid-Feb Late Feb/Mar
    Ice Harvest River Freezes Soft Opening Official Peak Crowds Festival Ends

    January 5, 1985 The first Ice and Snow festival started




  4. Zhaolin Park

    Zhaolin Park, established in 1906 in Harbin, China, is a historic 8.4-hectare park renamed in 1946 to honor anti-Japanese hero General Li Zhaolin. Famed as the birthplace of Harbin's ice lantern culture, it hosts an annual winter festival featuring intricate, illuminated ice sculptures alongside historic Russian-designed architecture and scenic summer gardens.

    1906 [Established] ---- 1946 [Renamed]




  5. Xuexiang National Forest Park

    Xuexiang National Forest Park (also known as China Snow Town or Xuexiang) is a premier winter wonderland located in Hailin, Mudanjiang City, Heilongjiang Province, China, about 3 to 5 hours southeast of Harbin. Originally named the Dahailin Forest Bureau Tourism Zone, it is famous for having a snow season that lasts up to seven months with snow piling up to 2 meters thick.

Good time to visit


A good time to visit Harbin is between end of November and start of March, but not suggest to visit during Januar to February, because of the winter break for Chinese school. The temperature will be around 5 degree - -13 degree fahrenheit. Around 9 hours daytime.
